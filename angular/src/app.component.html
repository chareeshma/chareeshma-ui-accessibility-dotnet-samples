Keyboard guide

A predictable path

  • Tab and Shift+Tab move between fields and buttons.
  • Enter submits the form for validation.
  • Invalid submission focuses an error summary with links to the fields.
  • Valid submission opens a review dialog and moves focus to its heading.
  • The native modal traps focus. Escape or Back to form closes it and restores focus.
